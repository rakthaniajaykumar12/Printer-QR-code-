# Epson EcoTank L3250 & Jio AirFiber Setup Guide

This guide explains how to connect your **Epson EcoTank L3250** all-in-one printer to your shop's Wi-Fi router or **Jio AirFiber** and configure your Android phone for reliable daily printing.

---

## 1. Network Requirements for Jio AirFiber / Wi-Fi Routers

The Epson EcoTank L3250 connects via **2.4 GHz Wi-Fi**.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Jio AirFiber 2.4 GHz vs 5 GHz:**
> Jio AirFiber routers broadcast both 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz bands under one Wi-Fi name (band steering) or two separate names (e.g., `JioFiber_2.4G` and `JioFiber_5G`).
> - Make sure 2.4 GHz is enabled on your router.
> - Connect your Android phone to the same shop Wi-Fi network that the printer uses.

---

## 2. Connecting Epson L3250 to Wi-Fi

You have two simple ways to connect the L3250 without a computer:

### Method A: Epson Smart Panel App (Recommended - Fastest from Phone)
1. Turn on your Epson EcoTank L3250 printer.
2. Install **Epson Smart Panel** from the Google Play Store on your Android phone.
3. Turn on **Bluetooth** and **Location (GPS)** on your phone (required by Android for initial Wi-Fi printer discovery).
4. Connect your phone to your shop's Wi-Fi (Jio AirFiber).
5. Open the **Epson Smart Panel** app.
6. The app's automated assistant will say *"No printer connected. Set up a new printer?"* Tap **Yes**.
7. Hold down the **Wi-Fi button** on the printer for 5 seconds until the **Wi-Fi and Wi-Fi Direct lights flash alternately**.
8. Select your shop's Wi-Fi name in the app and enter the Wi-Fi password.
9. Wait 60 seconds. The Wi-Fi indicator light on the L3250 will turn **solid green**. The printer is now permanently connected to your shop network!

---

### Method B: WPS Button Method (If router has a physical WPS button)
1. Press and hold the **Wi-Fi button** on the Epson L3250 for about 5 seconds until the **Wi-Fi light flashes green**.
2. Within 2 minutes, press the **WPS button** on your Jio AirFiber router / Wi-Fi router.
3. When the connection is established, the printer's Wi-Fi light stays **solid green**.

---

## 3. Recommended Android Printing Apps

Install both of these official apps on your Android phone from Google Play:

1. **Epson Smart Panel** (Primary):
   - Best user interface, live ink level monitoring, clean PDF viewer, and copy controls.
2. **Epson Print Enabler** (Background Plugin):
   - Installs Android's native print service so you can print directly from any Android file viewer via the standard Android "Share" or "Print" menu.
3. **Epson iPrint** (Alternative):
   - Useful lightweight backup app for older Android versions.

## 4. Pre-Opening Test Checklist

Before opening your shop to customers, perform these two test prints from your Android phone:

### Test 1: Black & White PDF Document Test
1. In your Android browser, open the Smart Xerox website.
2. Submit a sample 1-page B&W PDF order.
3. Open the Owner Dashboard, tap **Download File**.
4. Open the file in **Epson Smart Panel**.
5. Set:
   - **Color:** Grayscale / Monochrome
   - **Paper:** A4 Plain Paper
   - **Quality:** Standard
6. Tap **Print**.
7. Verify that the text is sharp and alignment is crisp.

### Test 2: Color Photo / Certificate Test
1. Submit a sample color photo or certificate image.
2. In Owner Dashboard, tap **Download File**.
3. Open in **Epson Smart Panel**.
4. Set:
   - **Color:** Color
   - **Paper:** A4 (or 4x6 Photo paper if requested)
5. Tap **Print**.
6. Verify cyan, magenta, and yellow ink levels produce accurate, smudge-free color.

## 5. Daily Xerox Shop Tips for Epson L3250

- **Keep Inks Topped Up:** The L3250 EcoTank uses 003 ink bottles. Do not let the ink level fall below the lower line on the tank window to prevent air bubbles in the print head.
- **Paper Tray Loading:** Load up to 100 sheets of 75-80 GSM A4 paper into the rear paper feeder. Adjust the edge guide firmly against the left edge.
- **Nozzle Check / Head Cleaning:** If text has faint white horizontal lines, run a nozzle check directly from the Epson Smart Panel app (Settings > Maintenance > Print Head Cleaning).
- **Wi-Fi IP Lease:** In your Jio AirFiber router settings, you can optionally assign a static/reserved IP to the Epson L3250 MAC address so it never changes IP after router restarts.
